About The Platform

Built for Graphic Era Students

Graphic Era University enforces a strict 75.00% attendance criterion for examination eligibility. gueattend was engineered to ensure no student is caught off guard.

🛡️ Debarment Edge-Case Protection

Standard portal roundings often mislead students when attendance sits at 74.8%. gueattend applies exact mathematical floor and ceiling calculations to ensure you are never debarred by a fraction of a percent.

🔐 Complete Multi-Tenant Privacy

Your portal password is encrypted with military-grade AES-256-GCM. Session cookies are kept in POSIX 0600 locked containers. Your attendance and chats are 100% private and isolated to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about how gueattend handles your college login, attendance calculations, and Telegram alerts.

Why is there no CAPTCHA on regular platform login? ▼
gueattend uses Google Firebase Authentication for your platform account, which uses signed cryptographic tokens. You only solve a CAPTCHA when connecting to the college portal (student.geu.ac.in) so our headless relay can extract your attendance securely!
How does the 1-Click Reconnect work when ERP sessions expire? ▼
When the college portal logs you out after a day or two, our background worker detects it. Because your password is saved encrypted in your AES-256 vault, you do not need to retype your password. You just enter one fresh CAPTCHA code and you are reconnected instantly!
How are the 75% attendance formulas calculated? ▼
For attendance ≥ 75%, safe skips are calculated as: floor((4A - 3T) / 3). For attendance < 75%, required catch-up classes are calculated as: max(1, ceil(3T - 4A)). View our interactive Calculator Guide for live proofs and interactive sliders.
Why do I need to verify my email address? ▼
Email verification ensures that only the genuine owner of the university email address can access the attendance dashboard, preventing spam accounts and safeguarding student privacy.
Is gueattend free to use for Graphic Era students? ▼
Yes! gueattend is 100% free for all students of Graphic Era University (GEU) and Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU).
