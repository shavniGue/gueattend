Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: November 2026 • Platform: gueattend

1. Identity & Authentication

gueattend uses Google Firebase Authentication for email registration, email verification, Google Sign-In, and password reset. Platform passwords are never stored in Supabase PostgreSQL.

2. University Portal Credentials & Session Isolation

Your GUeID binding is verified against your authenticated account to prevent unauthorized cross-account access. Any portal password stored for 1-click session reconnect is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before persistence, and CAPTCHA or Turnstile verification challenges always require direct human action.

3. Database Isolation & Row Level Security

All academic, attendance, timetable, examination, fee, and grievance records are isolated inside GUeAttend's dedicated Supabase PostgreSQL project with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies scoped by your verified Firebase UID and application role.

4. Telegram Bot Integration

Linking Telegram is optional and uses a single-use 15-minute cryptographic token. You can unlink your Telegram account at any time from your dashboard or Telegram settings.

5. Advertising & Public Pages

When configured by the operator, advertisements appear exclusively on public informational pages (such as the home page, calculator guide, and privacy policy). Advertisements are never placed inside private student academic records, grade/fee dashboards, or authentication login/registration screens.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or account recovery assistance, email support@gueattend.geu.ac.in.
